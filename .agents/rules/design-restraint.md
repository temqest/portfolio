# Rule: Design Restraint & Negative Space

When a layout feels "empty", has awkward side gaps, or leaves unused negative space on widescreen displays:

1. **NEVER Solve Emptiness with Content Bloat**:
   - Do NOT add decorative widgets, interactive test benches, extra cards, checklists, stats, or paragraphs simply to "fill the space".
   - Adding details to plug holes is a rookie anti-pattern (horror vacui) that creates visual clutter and distracts from the core message.

2. **Solve Negative Space Through Form, Not Volume**:
   - **Typographic Scale & Tension**: Let type breathe, scale display headlines with intention, and contrast massive structural markers/eyebrows against concise body copy.
   - **Break the Card Trap**: Question whether the content needs to be boxed in a floating card at all. Consider full-width editorial rules, panoramic horizontal pacing, broadsheet splits, or asymmetric alignment.
   - **Intentional Framing**: Use architectural grid lines, subtle alignment axes, or generous margins where whitespace feels confident and deliberate rather than like an accidental leftover gutter.
